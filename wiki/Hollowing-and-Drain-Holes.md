Hollow models use less resin, weigh less and crack less often.

## Hollowing
- **Wall thickness** — usually 1.5–3 mm.
- **Methods** — 2D (fast), 3D, and voxel 3D (most robust for complex models).
- **Blockers** — keep chosen areas solid. Automatic blockers protect thin parts.
- **Infill** — an inner lattice for strength.
- **Internal supports** — supports inside the hollow part.

## Drain holes
Every hollow part needs at least two holes: one lets resin out, one lets air in.
Copal can suggest positions. Place them low and in hidden spots.

## Checks
Copal warns about closed cavities with no holes (**resin traps**).
