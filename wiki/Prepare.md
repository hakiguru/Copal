## Overhang check (0.1)
In the **Prepare** step faces that point down more steeply than the overhang angle turn red: they
will need supports. The angle is in the **Supports** tab (45° by default). In other steps the
**Overhangs** button over the 3D view turns the colouring on.

## Supports *(0.3)*
Auto supports (light / medium / heavy), supports placed by hand that brace themselves to their
neighbours, editing and deleting each support. The settings are already in the **Supports** tab and
can be saved as presets. See [[Supports]].

## Hollowing and drain holes *(0.2)*
See [[Hollowing and drain holes|Hollowing-and-Drain-Holes]]. Wall thickness and holes are in the
**Hollow** tab.

## Auto orient *(0.2)*
Finds an angle with fewer islands, less support and smaller layer areas.

## Multi-plate, one-click prepare *(0.5)*
Several build plates in one project; a magic button that orients, supports and arranges.

## Measure and repair *(0.5)*
Distances, angles, wall thickness; fixes holes, flipped normals and broken geometry.
