Copal includes Blender-style editing, so small fixes don't need another program.

## Modes
- **Object mode** — move whole objects.
- **Edit mode** (`Tab`) — work with vertices (`1`), edges (`2`) and faces (`3`).

## Tools
- Select: click, box, lasso, linked, loops. `A` all, `Alt+A` none.
- Transform: `G` `R` `S` + axis + typed value.
- Extrude (`E`), inset, delete, merge, fill, subdivide, smooth, decimate.

## Booleans
Union, difference, intersection between objects.

## Cut, split and keys
- **Planar cut** — slice a model with a plane.
- **Split** — separate loose parts.
- **Assembly keys** — pins and holes so cut parts fit back together.

## Ironing
Smooths a chosen surface.

## Primitives and text
Add cubes, cylinders, spheres, plates; emboss or engrave text and logos.

## Modifiers
Non-destructive: mirror, array, boolean, decimate, hollow. Change them any time.
