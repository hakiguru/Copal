## Auto supports
Press **Auto supports** and pick **Light**, **Medium** or **Heavy**.
Every setting of the generator can be changed in the Inspector.

## Support types
- **Normal** — tip, shaft and base.
- **Branches / tree** — supports that merge to save resin.
- **Inline** — supports placed along a line.
- **Grid** — supports placed in a grid under a flat area.
- **Projection** — supports projected down from a painted area.
- **Bracing** — links between supports for strength. **Parenting** connects a support to another.

## Support painting
Paint areas that **need** supports or must stay **clean** (faces, visible details).

## Manual editing
Click to add, drag a tip to move it, `X` to delete, change size for one or many supports.

## Checks
- **Islands** — parts of a layer with nothing under them. Shown in red.
- **Proximity** — supports too close to the model.
- **Suction cups and resin traps** — closed cavities that pull or trap resin.

## Rafts
Flat, chamfered, skate-style and more. A raft helps the print stick to the plate.

## Export
Export the model **with supports** as STL or OBJ.
