## Slicing
Press **Slice**. Copal turns the scene into layer images for your printer.
- **Anti-aliasing**, **grey levels** and **blur** for smoother surfaces.
- **Bottom layers**, **transition layers**, wait times.
- **Two-stage lift and retract** (TSMC) on printers that support it.
- **XY compensation** for elephant foot and holes *(0.5)*.
- GPU slicing on supported graphics cards *(0.8)*.

## Preview
Scroll through layers with the slider. **Layer thickness preview** shows where problems are.

## Estimates
Print time, resin volume, weight and cost.

## Export
Pick the printer's format — see [[File formats|File-Formats]].
Save to a USB stick, or **send over Wi-Fi** to supported printers *(0.8)*.

## Open sliced files *(0.8)*
Open `.goo`, `.ctb` and other files, look at layers, fix settings, or convert between formats.
